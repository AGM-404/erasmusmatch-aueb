# Featured Erasmus course data (2026–27)

139 official-catalog course entries are seeded into the React app at startup. All are marked `unverified_candidate` **conservatively**, even when extracted from an official catalog: publication does not confirm exchange eligibility, the actual semester offer, or AUEB equivalence.

33 Ghent entries lack source course codes: generated `CATALOG-...` identifiers are internal placeholders, **not university course codes**. One Comenius duplicate source code is disambiguated using an internal `-ENTRY-2` suffix.

The seed is merged with locally imported user records (user imports take precedence). The 46 official partnership rows remain unchanged; the 6 featured universities remain the visible selection.

The 139 entries appear in the course updates section and candidate counts; they do not become eligible for approval drafts until independently verified and imported with the required evidence fields.

Source: `featured_course_import_2026_27.csv` from the project research dataset.
