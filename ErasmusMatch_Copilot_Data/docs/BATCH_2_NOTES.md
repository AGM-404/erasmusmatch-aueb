## Critical issue: Dauphine ECTS
The official Dauphine exchange rules say ECTS shown on ordinary degree syllabi may differ from ECTS awarded to incoming exchange students. The registration platform is authoritative for final exchange credits. Source: https://static3.dauphine.psl.eu/fileadmin/mediatheque/international/documents/plaquettes/dai_2026-2027_RULES_COURSE_CATALOGUE.pdf
